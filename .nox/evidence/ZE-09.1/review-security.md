# ZE-09.1 technical review and security

Root/Nox rendered review PASS at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 pixels, including unavailable. The font weight, two-column layout, media grid, summary proportions, fraction fills, cards, CTAs and source labels were compared with `new_interface/4.png`. No material open visual findings remain; owner visual approval is separate.

Findings corrected during review: screenshot crops originally included a sticky header and incomplete lazy images (capture method fixed to scroll/load/full-page/crop); original supplied Eurostile Black differed in weight (rejected after a successful lossless repair experiment); official OFL Michroma Regular self-hosted instead; summary/card vertical rhythm tightened while reserving identical loading/verified geometry; 200% enlarged text wraps naturally; rating numeral changed from low-contrast light gold to the heading gold.

Only Community portions of existing TSX/CSS and the existing Google hook changed. Location/Footer TSX suffix and all preexisting CSS are unchanged; hero, services/paint, process, product panels, Instagram API/hook, Google API/links, analytics/consent, SEO and dependencies are untouched. Machine checks: preservation.json.

`GET /api/google-reviews` is reused. No Places API, Maps JS SDK, additional backend, server credential, `.env` read, Cloud mutation, billing, paid tool, push or deployment. Provider payloads and real customer content were not logged. Browser fixtures live exclusively under ignored QA evidence and are never imported by runtime. TEST DATA route images are editorial photos, not assertions of official posts or reviews.

Normalized data rejects invalid averages/counts/IDs/URLs and unsafe author links. React renders comments as plain text, with truncation and a named accessible link to the full original Google review. Star fill is proportional and includes one accessible value. No filtering of negative reviews or invented fallback names, counts, stars, dates or comments. Institutional fallback cards are explicitly labeled.

Page-memory only: five-minute freshness, fifteen-minute maximum retention, no localStorage/IndexedDB/CDN persistence of reviews. Revalidation occurs only near-section entry/tab return or controlled manual refresh; no network interval polling. Shared requests, abort, timeout, backoff and permanent-failure manual recheck prevent storms. A temporary refresh error is visible and cannot preserve data indefinitely. Real Google verification remains ZE-09 WAITING_USER_ACTION.

Automated scoped axe WCAG2.2AA scans reported zero violations at desktop/mobile320/390. Image gradients make color-contrast checks incomplete; this is not represented as a full automatic contrast PASS. Conservative manual contrast calculation uses a worst-case #e9e9e9 surface for the decorative scene under two white overlays: title gold #a97b2d 3.11:1 (large text), body #555459 6.18:1, secondary #656168 4.99:1, action #805e27 4.87:1, metadata #68635c 4.90:1. The rating gold on the summary's #f5f2ec surface is 3.38:1. Cards/eyebrows have lighter backgrounds than this conservative scene bound. Decorative fraction-star fill #a87923 exceeds 3:1. Disabled cooldown text is inactive and excluded from WCAG contrast requirements. Focus, keyboard, secure external links, touch layout, reduced motion and 200% text/reflow were also tested separately. axe incomplete results remain retained in accessibility.json.

No field Core Web Vitals or real Google OAuth/API success is claimed. Performance evidence is a local comparable single sample per profile; no claim based on fixture screenshots proves real-provider activation.
