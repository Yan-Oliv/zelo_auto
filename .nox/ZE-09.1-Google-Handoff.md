## Refresh behavior

The first Google query remains lazy near its section. Valid responses render the official average, total and up to three comments. Page-memory data is fresh for five minutes, revalidated on nearby-section reentry or visible-tab focus, and discarded after fifteen minutes. There is no periodic network polling or browser persistence. Requests are deduplicated; responses remain no-store.

Configuration/authentication failures expose a manual recheck after a sixty-second cooldown. Once the server has been configured, that same mounted interface can recover without a full-page reload. Temporary refresh failures disclose that the visible data could not be refreshed; old data expires rather than being presented indefinitely. Changing data does not restart the hero or Instagram intro.
